Data notes. Rates are FENEGOSIDA figures collected by an automated daily job into prices.json; days marked interpolated were filled in between published rates. FENEGOSIDA publishes one rate per day, so each candle opens at the previous rate and closes at that day’s rate; wick lengths are estimated. The shaded 75 days before the first real rate are modeled, not real prices, and exist only to warm up EMA/MACD. 10 g = tola × 10 ÷ 11.6638. Rates refresh automatically each Nepali business day. Signals and the forecast are simple statistical rules, not financial advice.
